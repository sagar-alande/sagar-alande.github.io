KEY INSIGHTS

1

Pretraining is where the money goes. About 96% of DeepSeek-V3's GPU hours went to pretraining, while alignment shapes behavior at small cost.

2

Every resource grew fast. Data grew 52× from GPT-3 to Llama 3.1, and GPUs grew 43× from BLOOM to Llama 3.1.

3

Smarter design lowers cost. DeepSeek-V3 reported 11× fewer GPU hours than Llama 3.1 405B on similar data, helped by sparse experts and FP8. Its count covers only the final run on different chips.

4

Where you train changes the footprint. BLOOM and GPT-3 are similar in size, but BLOOM emitted 25 t against 552 t, thanks to newer chips and a cleaner grid.

HOW TO READ THE NUMBERS

Open models publish GPU hours and emissions. Closed models such as GPT-4 and Claude publish little, so their figures come from executives or outside estimates.

GPU hours compare only roughly because chips differ in speed, and costs usually cover only the final run.

Title

Interactive Learning of Machine Learning Concepts using AI Coach

What is it?

This activity involved interacting with the Machine Learning Training Methods Coach (AI chatbot) to explore and understand core machine learning concepts such as neural networks, SVM, and CNN. The learning was carried out through a question-driven approach, where concepts were built gradually using guided responses from the chatbot.

Objective

The objective was to gain a clear and intuitive understanding of machine learning concepts by actively engaging with the AI coach. The focus was on breaking down complex topics into simpler parts and improving understanding through repeated questioning and refinement.

Process

The learning session followed a step-by-step interactive approach where concepts were explored through targeted questioning and progressive refinement.

1. Initiating Concept with Direct Questions

I began the session by asking direct conceptual questions such as “What is neural network in machine learning?” The response provided a high-level definition, which helped establish a basic understanding of the topic.

2. Breaking Down the Concept into Components

Instead of moving forward immediately, I focused on understanding the internal structure by asking about neural networks, nodes, and hidden layers. This step helped isolate the key parts and showed how each contributes to the overall system.

3. Simplifying the Explanation

After receiving a technical explanation, I refined the learning by requesting simpler interpretations, such as asking for explanations suitable for someone unfamiliar with machine learning. This improved conceptual clarity.

4. Using Examples for Better Understanding

To connect theory with intuition, I explored examples such as recognizing handwritten numbers and understanding how systems identify patterns in images. This made the learning more concrete and easier to visualize.

5. Iterative Refinement Through Follow-ups

The learning was not linear. After each response, I asked follow-up questions to clarify missing points and refine the explanation. This ensured the concepts became more accurate and aligned with my understanding.

6. Structuring the Learning into Essay Format

Once I understood the concept, I shifted to organizing it into a coherent and structured explanation. This helped convert fragmented understanding into a complete written response.

7. Enhancing with Analogies and Comparisons

To deepen understanding, I used analogies and real-life comparisons, such as student learning examples. This made it easier to understand how learning, feedback, and improvement happen in neural networks.

8. Validating Understanding Through Rewriting

Finally, I reviewed and refined the explanation by rewriting parts in my own words and requesting further improvements. This ensured that the concept was not only understood but could also be communicated clearly.

Sample Prompts Used

Let’s understand mathematical concepts for SVM first

So SVM is supervised learning model. Can you differentiate between all available supervised models by definition and best fit scenario?

So we are learning unsupervised learning right. We dont have labels then how the data is divided into train and test?

Explain CNN in layman terms

Tools

Machine Learning Training Methods Coach (AI Chatbot)

Value

This activity demonstrates a self-driven and analytical approach to learning complex technical concepts. It highlights the ability to break down abstract ideas, engage in structured questioning, and build understanding progressively.

The process reflects problem-solving ability, curiosity, and communication skill, along with the capability to transform difficult machine learning ideas into clear and intuitive explanations.

Reflection — What I Learned

This School AI chatbot session helped me move from simply recognizing machine learning terms to understanding when and why different methods are used. Instead of passively reading definitions, I learned by asking follow-up questions, comparing concepts, and refining unclear areas step by step. That made the learning more practical and helped me build stronger judgment around model selection, evaluation, and interpretation.

What I Learned

  • Understood the difference between supervised and unsupervised learning and when each approach is appropriate.
  • Learned how SVM works through margins, support vectors, and kernels such as linear, polynomial, and RBF.
  • Strengthened my understanding of model training basics like train/test split, random_state, and simple evaluation logic.
  • Learned how Lasso helps with regularization and feature selection.
  • Built a clearer understanding of neural networks, including nodes, hidden layers, forward pass, loss, and backpropagation.
  • Learned the basics of CNNs, especially how filters, pooling, and feature extraction work in image-related tasks.
  • Understood major dimensionality reduction techniques like PCA, t-SNE, and LDA, along with where each is useful.
  • Learned the difference between clustering and anomaly detection, including when their goals overlap.
  • Improved my understanding of unsupervised evaluation using ideas like silhouette score, reconstruction error, and appropriate splitting strategies.

Key Skills Developed

  • Conceptual understanding: I became better at distinguishing major machine learning paradigms and choosing suitable model families.
  • Analytical interpretation: I improved my ability to interpret PCA explained variance, t-SNE perplexity, and SVM kernel choices.
  • Practical ML judgment: I learned why steps like standardization and train_test_split matter before applying certain models.
  • Model evaluation mindset: I gained more clarity on selecting basic supervised and unsupervised evaluation metrics.
  • Problem framing: I developed better judgment around when to reduce dimensions, cluster data, or focus on anomaly detection.
  • Communication: I improved at explaining technical models and trade-offs in simpler and more intuitive language.

Reflection

  • This activity showed me that learning machine learning becomes much easier when complex topics are explored through guided questioning instead of trying to absorb everything at once.
  • The School AI chatbot was most useful when I used it interactively — asking clarifying questions, comparing related ideas, and revisiting confusing points until the logic became clear.
  • I also realized that understanding a model is not just about knowing its definition, but about knowing when to use it, what assumptions it makes, and how to evaluate whether it is working properly.
  • Overall, this session helped me build a more structured way of learning technical concepts and gave me more confidence to continue into deeper machine learning topics.
“This learning experience showed me that machine learning is not just about memorizing models. It is about understanding the problem first, choosing the right method for the context, and being able to explain the reasoning clearly.”

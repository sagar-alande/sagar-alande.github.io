TripForge

An AI-powered travel planning chatbot designed to create structured, personalized itineraries for trips within the United States.

Explore TripForge

What is it?

TripForge is an AI-powered travel-planning chatbot built to help users generate structured and personalized itineraries for travel within the United States. It supports road trips, air travel, and RV/camper journeys by providing destination suggestions, day-by-day planning, travel logistics, and stay options.

Objective

The goal of TripForge was to create a reliable travel companion that simplifies trip planning without overwhelming the user. By narrowing the scope to U.S. travel and using structured outputs, the chatbot was designed to produce realistic and actionable itineraries in a clear format.

Process

The project was designed as a guided conversational system where user intent, prompt instructions, and structured outputs work together to generate useful travel plans.

1. Architecture

The solution follows a RAG-style pattern combined with a conversational AI model: user input flows through prompt instructions and a structured knowledge approach before generating a travel plan.

2. Design Decisions

The chatbot was limited to U.S. travel to reduce complexity and improve response relevance. Outputs were structured to include summaries, day-wise itineraries, travel estimates, and suggestions for activities and accommodations.

3. GPT Configuration

The system was built using a custom GPT setup with role instructions, behavior guidelines, travel-oriented conversation starters, and repeated testing to refine response quality and clarity.

4. Prompt Engineering

Prompt instructions were used to make responses more consistent and useful. The chatbot was guided to ask clarifying questions, generate structured itineraries, and avoid giving unreliable real-time information.

5. Rules and Constraints

To keep the system realistic, TripForge was designed not to provide real-time flight prices, booking services, guaranteed visa guidance, or invented hotel reviews. It also encourages users to verify critical travel details before booking.

6. Conversation Flow

The chatbot first captures the travel goal, then asks follow-up questions such as budget, duration, and travel style. Based on those inputs, it generates a structured itinerary and updates the plan as the user refines preferences.

Example Interaction

User: Plan a 5-day road trip from Chicago to Nashville.

TripForge: Generates route suggestions, daily stops, attractions, and hotel options in a structured format.

User: Plan a 4-day air trip to Seattle with a moderate budget.

TripForge: Provides flight travel notes, hotel suggestions, and daily activities based on the given constraints.

Tools Used

  • ChatGPT / GPT Builder
  • Prompt Engineering
  • RAG-style Knowledge Base
  • Design Tools for branding and logo creation

Value

TripForge demonstrates how conversational AI, structured prompting, and guided interactions can work together to create a travel assistant that feels practical, personalized, and easy to use.

It reduces planning friction, keeps travel logic consistent, and creates a strong foundation for future features such as live APIs, interactive maps, and deeper personalization.

Reflection — What I Learned

Building TripForge taught me that a useful AI product is not just about generating answers. It is about designing a system that understands the user’s goal, works within clear boundaries, and produces responses that feel structured, realistic, and trustworthy.

Key Learnings

  • Clear constraints improve reliability and keep outputs more focused.
  • Structured prompting has a major impact on consistency and usefulness.
  • Clarifying questions lead to better personalization and better itineraries.
  • Trust matters more than sounding overly confident in travel planning.
  • Good conversational flow is as important as the final generated answer.

Key Skills Developed

  • Prompt engineering: designing instructions for structure and consistency.
  • Conversation design: shaping the flow to gather the right user inputs.
  • Constraint design: defining limits to reduce hallucinations and false claims.
  • Product thinking: focusing on usability, trust, and user experience.
  • AI reliability mindset: balancing creativity with accuracy and practical value.

Reflection

  • This project showed me that even a simple chatbot becomes stronger when it is designed like a system, not treated like a single prompt.
  • I learned that useful outputs depend on how well the chatbot gathers context before generating results.
  • I also realized that setting boundaries improves user trust more than trying to answer everything.
  • Overall, TripForge helped me think more deeply about structure, usability, and trust in conversational AI products.
“Building TripForge taught me that a strong AI product is not just about generating responses. It is about guiding the user well, respecting constraints, and producing answers that are useful enough to trust.”

Future Improvements

Future enhancements can include real-time travel APIs for flights, hotels, and weather, interactive maps, stronger personalization, and mobile deployment to make the experience more dynamic and practical.
